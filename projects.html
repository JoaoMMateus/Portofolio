HOME SERVER HUB
Ongoing
A self-hosted home server that centralises home automation and media services. The main machine runs Alpine Linux (32-bit) to make use of hardware I already had.
The project covers several areas, each at a different stage:
  • Media - A Jellyfin server connected to the TV streams content from the Alpine machine over a shared network drive.
  • Garden irrigation - Sector-based watering control via an ESP32 driving an electrovalve, managed by the Alpine machine.
  • Gate access - Planned OCR pipeline: a camera reads number plates and automates door open/close. On hold until the door/gate is installed, as it was planned for my parents new house.

ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
WUMPUS
2017
This project aims to develop an artificial intelligence that will participate in the game called "The World of Wumpus". Artificial intelligence will have to move around the playground to reach the treasure without getting caught by the Wumpus. This one is a monster that defends its cave, this one being the playground. In this work the visual appearance was not the main goal to ask. The idea was to allow the AI to move according to the data it receives by moving from box to box. The smell of Wumpus allows the AI to recognize its presence on one of the adjacent boxes. The flicker of gold signals the presence of the treasure in the box and thus indicates to the AI the achievement of its objective.
Augmented Reality
2018
In this Reconstructed Professional Situation (MSPR), the main goal was to display a 3D model associated with an image that children could color. The application should therefore be able to retrieve the colors present on the drawing and apply them to the 3D model. Being intended for a very young audience, we therefore thought of adding an additional functionality: interaction with the 3D model. To illustrate this idea, we added a target that the 3D model will follow.
