WUMPUS
2017
This project aims to develop an artificial intelligence that will participate in the game called "The World of Wumpus". Artificial intelligence will have to move around the playground to reach the treasure without getting caught by the Wumpus. This one is a monster that defends its cave, this one being the playground. In this work the visual appearance was not the main goal to ask. The idea was to allow the AI to move according to the data it receives by moving from box to box. The smell of Wumpus allows the AI to recognize its presence on one of the adjacent boxes. The flicker of gold signals the presence of the treasure in the box and thus indicates to the AI the achievement of its objective.
